##### Abstract

This paper examines the robustness of proposed determinants of civil conflict in Colombia. Using a battery of empirical tests, we assess how sensitive existing findings are to changes in specification, sample, and measurement. Our results inform the debate on the relative importance of grievance versus greed in sustaining armed conflict.
