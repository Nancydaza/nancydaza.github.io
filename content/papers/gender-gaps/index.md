##### Abstract

This paper provides a comprehensive cross-country analysis of gender earning gaps using a non-parametric decomposition approach applied to data from 64 countries. We document the extent and structure of gender gaps in earnings, and examine the role of differences in worker and job characteristics in explaining observed gaps.
